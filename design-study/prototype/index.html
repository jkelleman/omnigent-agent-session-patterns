Supervising agents from your phone

A working prototype of a mobile supervision view for a meta-harness like Omnigent. Five coding sessions are running while you're away from your desk. The view answers two questions: what needs you, and what happens if you don't respond.

Tap a session in the phone, or jump to a state:

Open the desktop orchestration view, built with Omnigent's own design tokens, including watch, co-drive, and fork.

An independent design study. Databricks has not reviewed or endorsed it. The sessions and names are made up. Polly is Omnigent's example orchestrator agent.
