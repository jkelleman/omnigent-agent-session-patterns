# About these images

The images in this folder come from [Omnigent](https://github.com/omnigent-ai/omnigent), © Databricks, licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/omnigent-ai/omnigent/blob/main/LICENSE). They're used here to document Omnigent's current interface for design critique.

- `desktop.png`, `mobile-session.png`, and `policy-trust-model.png` come from Omnigent's `docs/images/`. The desktop image and the trust model are resized.
- All the other screenshots are Omnigent's own visual-test reference images, rendered from its Storybook stories, in `tests/e2e_ui/visual/snapshots/`. They show the real components with example data.
- `sidebar-badges.png` enlarges four of those badge images, arranges them in a grid, and labels the state each one shows. `host-offline.png` is cropped.

This design study is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Databricks.
